# Marketplace Review

Evaluate product browsing, checkout, subscriptions, licenses, and supplier API readiness.

## Check

- Product discovery
- Search/filter usability
- Product comparison
- Checkout clarity
- Subscription purchases
- License management
- Account-based purchasing
- Supplier API product data consistency
- Cart clarity
- Error recovery

## Questions

- Can users find and compare products efficiently?
- Are checkout steps and totals clear?
- Are subscription and license terms understandable?
- Does supplier-fed content follow HACIEN standards?
- Are procurement and account purchasing contexts clear?
