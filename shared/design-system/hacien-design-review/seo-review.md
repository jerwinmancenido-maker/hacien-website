# SEO Review

Use for public website and indexable marketplace pages.

## Check

- Metadata
- Heading structure
- Internal linking
- Structured data opportunities
- Page performance
- Image alt text
- Canonical intent
- Crawlable content
- Product/category content clarity

## Questions

- Can search engines understand the page topic?
- Does the heading structure match the content hierarchy?
- Are internal links useful and descriptive?
- Are product, solution, and RFQ paths discoverable?
- Are performance choices likely to hurt ranking or crawling?

Do not apply SEO requirements to private ERP screens unless they affect internal search or content organization.
