# Customer Portal Review

Evaluate self-service usability.

## Check

- Account management
- Quotations
- Orders
- Subscriptions
- Invoices
- Support tickets
- Renewals
- Document access
- Status clarity
- Notifications

## Questions

- Can customers find what they need without contacting support?
- Are quotation, order, invoice, and subscription states clear?
- Are support and renewal paths visible?
- Are sensitive account actions clear and safe?
- Does the portal feel connected to HACIEN website and ERP?
