# Accessibility

Accessibility applies to every HACIEN product.

## Requirements

- Follow WCAG AA where practical.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Maintain semantic HTML or platform-native semantics.
- Preserve keyboard navigation.
- Provide visible focus states.
- Maintain readable contrast.
- Do not rely on color alone.
- Do not block interaction with animation.

## Forms

Forms require labels, accessible errors, clear required/optional states, logical tab order, and stable validation behavior.

## Tables and Reports

Use proper table semantics where applicable. Preserve header relationships, sortable labels, status text, and accessible chart labels.

## Dialogs and Drawers

Trap focus, restore focus, support escape behavior, and expose accessible names.

## Mobile

Respect touch target size, screen reader labels, reduced motion, and readable density.
