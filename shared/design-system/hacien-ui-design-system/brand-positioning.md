# Brand Positioning

HACIEN is a procurement-driven technology company. The design system must communicate clarity, trust, technical accuracy, supplier coordination, quotation discipline, delivery accountability, and future ERP-enabled operations.

## Ecosystem Promise

Users should immediately recognize HACIEN across public website, ERP, portal, marketplace, supplier product pages, and future mobile. The products may have different workflows, but they should feel like one system.

## Personality

HACIEN should feel:

- professional
- modern
- precise
- trustworthy
- enterprise-grade
- helpful
- calm under complexity

Avoid:

- generic reseller styling
- gaming visuals
- portfolio layouts
- loud startup exaggeration
- decorative animation without operational purpose

## Product Surface Balance

The same identity should flex by context:

- Website: credibility, storytelling, conversion, RFQ confidence.
- ERP: operational speed, accuracy, density, accountability.
- Portal: self-service clarity and confidence.
- Marketplace: product discovery, checkout clarity, account purchasing.
- Mobile: fast touch-first actions and simplified navigation.
