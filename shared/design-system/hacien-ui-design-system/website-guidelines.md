# Website Guidelines

The public website should prioritize trust, conversion, storytelling, premium first impression, SEO, RFQ flow, product browsing, and company credibility.

## Goals

- Explain why HACIEN exists.
- Make procurement expertise obvious.
- Guide users toward RFQ and contact.
- Make product and solution browsing credible.
- Support SEO without sacrificing design quality.
- Feel premium, not flashy.

## Layout

Use storytelling sections, strong hierarchy, readable long-form content, and clear CTAs. Keep the first viewport polished and brand-forward.

## Motion

Allowed:

- hero reveal
- section reveal
- marquee
- circular CTA
- animated cards
- subtle gradient backgrounds
- animated workflow
- spotlight/hover cards

Motion must not cover text, delay CTA access, or harm performance.

## RFQ and Product Browsing

RFQ flow must stay clear and fast. Product browsing should support comparison, category clarity, and quote intent.

## Framework Guidance

If the website uses Next.js or another framework, follow that project's conventions. Keep reusable UI and React Bits wrappers in the website's shared component area, not inside individual route files.
