# ERP Guidelines

HACIEN ERP should prioritize speed, readability, operational clarity, data density, keyboard-friendly workflows, low cognitive load, and accessibility.

## Goals

- Help users complete work quickly.
- Make status, ownership, and next action obvious.
- Support procurement, quotation, inventory, billing, approval, delivery, reports, and admin workflows.
- Reduce ambiguity in data-heavy screens.

## Layout

Use dense but readable layouts:

- compact headers
- predictable sidebars/topbars
- clear module navigation
- stable tables
- focused panels
- visible filters
- minimal decoration

## Motion

Allowed:

- subtle loading states
- hover/focus states
- empty states
- dashboard card reveal
- workflow status transitions
- progress feedback

Avoid expressive marketing animations inside ERP workflows. No marquee, circular CTA, large hero reveals, decorative gradients, or flashy animated cards in daily work screens.

## Forms and Tables

Forms and tables are first-class ERP components. They must be fast, keyboard-friendly, accessible, and stable.

## Reports

Reports should be sober, export-ready, and trustworthy. Avoid decorative animation around financial, procurement, or inventory data.
