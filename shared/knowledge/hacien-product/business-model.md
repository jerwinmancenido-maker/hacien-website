# Business Model

HACIEN's business model combines technology supply, services, projects, subscriptions, renewals, and future platform workflows.

## Revenue and Operating Areas

- hardware
- software
- services
- projects
- subscriptions
- support
- renewals
- marketplace
- ERP-enabled operations
- customer portal self-service

## How They Work Together

The website creates demand and trust. RFQs and customer accounts become structured opportunities. Marketplace and supplier APIs can support direct checkout and automated product data where appropriate. HACIEN ERP coordinates internal workflows. Customer Portal exposes status, documents, orders, subscriptions, support, renewals, and projects back to customers.

The business model should stay procurement-first even as direct checkout and marketplace capabilities grow.
