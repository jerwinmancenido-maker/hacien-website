# Brand Positioning

HACIEN should be perceived as:

- professional
- reliable
- modern
- technical
- trustworthy
- organized
- enterprise-ready
- helpful

## Tone

Use clear, practical, confident language. Explain how HACIEN works rather than making exaggerated claims.

Avoid:

- hype
- vague superiority claims
- flashy tone
- aggressive sales language
- generic IT reseller wording
- generic SaaS buzzwords

HACIEN should feel like a company that understands procurement reality, technical validation, supplier coordination, and implementation handover.
