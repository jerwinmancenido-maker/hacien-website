# Mission and Vision

## Mission

HACIEN's mission is to simplify IT procurement by providing trustworthy technology solutions, structured supplier coordination, and digital workflows that reduce manual processes and improve customer experience.

## Vision

HACIEN evolves into a connected digital ecosystem where customer demand, supplier data, internal ERP workflows, marketplace purchasing, customer self-service, and AI support work together.

Long-term ecosystem:

```text
Website
-> Customer Account
-> Marketplace
-> Supplier API
-> HACIEN ERP
-> Customer Portal
-> AI
```

## Vision Principles

- Make technology procurement easier to understand.
- Reduce duplicate encoding and manual follow-up.
- Improve quotation accuracy and speed.
- Connect customer-facing and internal workflows.
- Use automation where practical.
- Keep humans accountable for customer trust and complex decisions.
