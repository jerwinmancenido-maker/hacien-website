# AI Roadmap

AI capabilities are future roadmap items unless verified.

## Future Capabilities

- BOQ analysis
- requirement extraction
- quotation drafting
- procurement assistant
- supplier recommendation
- customer support
- reporting
- forecasting

## AI Philosophy

AI should reduce manual work, extract structure from messy requirements, support faster quotation preparation, and help users understand procurement choices.

AI should not remove accountability from supplier validation, customer approval, pricing commitments, compliance, or final business decisions.
