# Company Overview

HACIEN Information Technology Solutions is an IT solutions, procurement, and project implementation company.

HACIEN helps organizations plan, source, procure, implement, and support technology requirements. The company coordinates technology procurement, supplier management, implementation support, documentation, and customer relationships.

## What HACIEN Is

- IT solutions provider
- Procurement coordination partner
- Supplier canvassing and sourcing partner
- Technology project support partner
- Future connected digital platform

## What HACIEN Is Not

- Not simply an online store
- Not simply an ERP vendor
- Not a generic reseller catalog
- Not an accounting-first platform
- Not a collection of disconnected modules

HACIEN's core business is procurement-first: understanding requirements, validating technical options, coordinating suppliers, preparing quotations, managing purchase flow, and supporting delivery through customer handover.
