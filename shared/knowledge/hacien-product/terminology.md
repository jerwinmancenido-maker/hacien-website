# Terminology

Use these terms consistently.

## Official Terms

- Lead: an initial customer or prospect signal.
- Opportunity: a qualified business requirement being worked.
- RFQ: request for quotation.
- Quotation: customer-facing commercial offer.
- Supplier Canvass: supplier sourcing and comparison activity.
- Purchase Plan: internal procurement plan based on approved requirements.
- Purchase Order: order issued to supplier.
- Receiving: recording goods or services received.
- Delivery: handover or fulfillment to customer.
- Sales Invoice: billing document for customer sale.
- Official Receipt: receipt document for payment.
- Customer Portal: customer-facing self-service account layer.
- Marketplace: future product discovery and direct purchase/subscription layer.
- Platform: connected HACIEN ecosystem across website, ERP, portal, marketplace, supplier APIs, and AI.

## Language Rule

Use procurement-specific terms instead of generic e-commerce language when the workflow requires RFQ, quotation, approval, procurement, delivery, and support.
