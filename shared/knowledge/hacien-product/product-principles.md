# Product Principles

Use these principles for product decisions.

- Procurement before paperwork.
- Minimize clicks.
- Reduce duplicate encoding.
- Customer-first workflows.
- Automation where practical.
- Transparency.
- Auditability.
- Simplicity.
- Scalability.
- Reusability.
- Preserve context from lead to support.
- Prefer connected workflows over disconnected modules.

## Decision Rule

When a feature conflicts with procurement clarity, auditability, or customer trust, redesign the feature.
