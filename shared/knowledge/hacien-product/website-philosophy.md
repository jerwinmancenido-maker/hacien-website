# Website Philosophy

The HACIEN website is not merely marketing.

It is the public entry point into HACIEN's procurement ecosystem.

## Purpose

- Explain HACIEN's procurement-first value.
- Build customer trust.
- Support product and solution discovery.
- Capture RFQs and contact inquiries.
- Prepare future customer accounts.
- Eventually connect directly to ERP workflows.

## Content Rule

Website content should explain how HACIEN works, why procurement is structured, and how customers move from requirement to quotation and fulfillment.

Avoid generic IT company language and exaggerated claims.
