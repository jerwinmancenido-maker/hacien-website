# Customer Personas

## Government Buyers

Goals:

- compliant procurement
- clear specifications
- approved equivalent support
- quotation documentation
- delivery coordination

Pain points:

- inconsistent supplier documents
- unclear technical specifications
- strict timelines
- procurement process complexity

## Private Companies and SMEs

Goals:

- reliable IT supply
- practical pricing
- fast quotation
- setup and delivery support
- renewal and support continuity

Pain points:

- fragmented vendors
- manual follow-ups
- unclear warranty and availability
- limited internal IT resources

## Schools

Goals:

- classroom technology
- network reliability
- licensing support
- project delivery planning

Pain points:

- budget cycles
- multiple campus needs
- procurement documentation

## Healthcare

Goals:

- reliable infrastructure
- secure systems
- responsive support
- continuity of operations

Pain points:

- downtime sensitivity
- device and network reliability
- data/security concerns

## Manufacturing and Retail

Goals:

- operational technology continuity
- branch/site support
- inventory and endpoint reliability
- network and security stability

Pain points:

- distributed sites
- urgent replacements
- vendor coordination

## Roles

- IT Managers: need technical fit, reliability, and implementation support.
- Procurement Officers: need documentation, comparison, and supplier-ready quotations.
- Business Owners: need confidence, cost control, and outcomes.
- Operations Managers: need delivery coordination and continuity.
