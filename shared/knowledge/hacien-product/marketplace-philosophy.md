# Marketplace Philosophy

The future HACIEN Marketplace supports products that can be discovered, purchased, subscribed to, renewed, or fulfilled digitally.

## Future Marketplace Areas

- software
- subscriptions
- digital products
- supplier API-powered catalog
- automatic fulfillment
- license management
- renewals

## RFQ vs Direct Checkout

Not every product should be direct checkout.

RFQ-driven products:

- require technical review
- require supplier canvass
- have variable availability or pricing
- involve projects, delivery, or services
- need customer approval before procurement

Direct-checkout products:

- have clear pricing
- have clear license/subscription terms
- can be fulfilled reliably
- do not require project scoping

The marketplace must preserve procurement workflows instead of replacing them.
