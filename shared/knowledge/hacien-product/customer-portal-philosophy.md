# Customer Portal Philosophy

The Customer Portal is a future self-service layer for customers.

## Future Capabilities

- accounts
- quotations
- orders
- invoices
- subscriptions
- support
- renewals
- projects
- documents

## Purpose

The portal should help customers understand status, retrieve documents, request support, manage renewals, and continue procurement conversations without starting from scratch.

## Rule

Do not claim the portal is fully implemented unless verified. Treat these as roadmap capabilities where not yet built.
