# ERP Philosophy

HACIEN ERP is not a generic ERP.

It is procurement-first.

## Core Ideas

- Opportunity Workspace is the heart of the system.
- Supplier Canvassing is a key differentiator.
- Everything revolves around projects and customer requirements.
- ERP workflows should reduce duplicate encoding.
- ERP should connect lead, opportunity, RFQ, quotation, supplier canvass, purchase plan, order, receiving, delivery, invoice, and support.

## Not Accounting-First

Accounting matters, but HACIEN ERP should not be designed as an accounting-first ERP. Procurement and customer requirement workflows come first, with financial documents generated from the operational flow.

## Operator Experience

The ERP should help users work faster with auditability, fewer clicks, less duplicate data entry, clear statuses, and practical automation.
