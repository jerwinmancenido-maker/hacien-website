# Examples

## Quotation Page

Request: create or redesign a quotation page.

The AI should understand:

- Opportunity-first workflow
- RFQ before checkout when appropriate
- customer approval before procurement
- Customer Portal integration potential
- ERP integration potential
- quotation connects to supplier canvass and purchase plan

## Product Page

Request: create or redesign a product page.

The AI should understand:

- some products are RFQ-only
- some software products may allow direct checkout
- supplier API may eventually provide pricing and fulfillment
- availability should not be overclaimed
- product content should support procurement decisions

## ERP Opportunity Workspace

Request: build an opportunity feature.

The AI should understand:

- Opportunity Workspace is central to HACIEN ERP
- supplier canvassing is a key differentiator
- customer requirements, quotation, purchase planning, and support should remain connected

## Marketplace Checkout

Request: build checkout.

The AI should understand:

- checkout is appropriate only for products with clear pricing and fulfillment
- subscriptions and licenses need renewal and account visibility
- checkout must still connect to ERP and Customer Portal concepts

## AI Quotation Assistant

Request: add AI quotation drafting.

The AI should understand:

- AI can extract requirements and draft structure
- AI must not invent pricing, availability, supplier commitments, or approvals
- human validation remains required
