# Procurement Workflow

The HACIEN workflow is central to the business.

```text
Requirement
-> Technical Review
-> Supplier Canvass
-> Quotation
-> Customer Approval
-> Sales Order
-> Procurement
-> Receiving
-> Delivery
-> Invoice
-> Support
```

## Why This Matters

This workflow keeps requirements, supplier inputs, customer approvals, purchasing, delivery, billing, and support connected.

HACIEN should not be designed as a generic checkout-first store. Many technology purchases require review, sourcing, quotation, approval, and documentation before fulfillment.

## Product Implications

- RFQ is central for many products and projects.
- Supplier canvassing is a differentiator.
- Technical review improves trust and accuracy.
- Customer approval must be explicit.
- Delivery and invoice workflows must connect to procurement history.
- Support should know the context of what was sold, delivered, and approved.
